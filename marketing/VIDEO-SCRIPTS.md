All three videos use large on-screen captions, so they work with the sound off (most social viewers watch muted). To add a voiceover or music:

1. Open **CapCut** (free, desktop or phone) → **New project** → import the video.
2. **Audio → Sounds** → pick a calm "tech / corporate" track → set its volume to about 20%.
3. For a voiceover, either:
   - **Record yourself:** Audio → Record, reading the script below, or
   - **AI voice:** Text → paste a line → **Text to speech** → pick a voice → drag each line under its scene.
4. **Export** → 1080p, 30 fps.

## Tutorial voiceover (2 min 55 s)

Each on-screen step has a caption; read it or expand it:

1. **Sign up:** Go to proofmyai.com and click Start free. Enter your company name, email and a password. Your first project is created automatically.
2. **Dashboard:** This is your dashboard. The checklist on the right shows what to set up next.
3. **Knowledge base and audit:** Open Chatbot audits. First upload your help articles: your FAQ, prices and policies. This is the truth your bot must follow. Then export your chats from Intercom, Tidio, Zendesk or your own bot as a CSV file, upload it, and click Start audit.
4. **Results:** In about a minute you get your accuracy score and a fix list showing which help article to update first. Each answer shows what the customer asked, what the bot said and why it's wrong. Click thumbs up if the verdict is right, or choose the correct one. That trains your own AI model.
5. **Rules:** Add your own rules, like words your bot must never say.
6. **Client report:** Agencies can share a branded client report, or save it as a PDF. Your client sees the score, what to fix first and every problem answer, without logging in.
7. **Live tracking:** Open Live tracking to watch your chatbot, agents and workflows in real time. Connect your bot with one HTTP call. Green means working, red means a problem, and you get an alert.
8. **Chatbot tests:** Chatbot tests asks your bot important questions every night. Add a question and the facts a correct answer must include, then click Run tests.
9. **AI agents:** Copy the code for your language and add it where your agent finishes.
10. **n8n & Make:** Paste your n8n address and API key to monitor every workflow.
11. **Alerts:** In Settings, add your Slack or Discord webhook or your email to get instant alerts.
12. **Done:** One health score for all your AI. ProofMyAI keeps watching 24/7.
